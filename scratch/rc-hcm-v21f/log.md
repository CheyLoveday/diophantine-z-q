The geometric level-order equality is an actual definition in the supplied coordinates. The trace found no hypothesis saying that the endomorphism ring of a quotient is the right order of its ideal. In the current path, enhanced Hom lattices of the coprime comparison quotients are built directly with the reverse isogeny and their local rings are read using Tate chains. The only assertion that all pairs correspond to ideal classes is the inherited coarse-model equivalence. Its domain includes all ideal classes, while the framed-reference result is a theorem about one labelled torus orbit. An explicit family of maps that identifies actual cyclic enhancements with nested ideals, and proves descent to isomorphism classes in both directions, would be needed to replace the global field.
