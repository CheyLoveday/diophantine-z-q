# Sizing and results

- VERIFIED: The base is the final scoped handoff. The moving block and unary blocks are genuine weight-two modular forms with separately specified characters (`evenWeightTwo`); `joinedForm` combines them at `movingLevel P U`. Each unary level `maskedLevel P * j.val` divides that level. No common level across different tested sets is needed.
- VERIFIED: The open input to `evenExpansion_joined_scoped` is `∀ n, n ≠ 0 → κ (algebraMap X.O X.K (A.coeff n (X.F U))) = (qExpansion 1 (joinedForm hS i U hU hodd)).coeff n`. It yields `∀ n, n ≠ 0 → A.coeff n (X.F U) = E.ringMap (timesTheta (fixedH C₀ i U hU) n)`. The unscoped `evenExpansion` uses the same positive-coordinate equality with a supplied form and gives `timesTheta (evenH hS A C i U) n`.
- VERIFIED: The odd `Inventory` stores primitive source levels, old degrees, source characters and independent coefficient columns. `HasWeightTwoExpansion` refers to genuine forms in their own character space. `spansAt_of_complex` and `coordinates_of_span` descend supplied complex spanning to supported field coordinates; `evenVector_eval` gives the Fourier expansion of their scaled combination. Odd `Blocks.coeff_F` evaluates the seed by construction. The even `coordinate_match_of_seed_scoped` replaces that construction with two identities, `∀ w n, X.coefK (ι w) (A.index n) = (Y.toBasis hc).eval n.val w` and `(X.F U).val = ι (evenSeed …)`. A preselected abstract carrier need satisfy neither identity.
- VERIFIED: Tex 9647–9654 and 9754–9764 require separate characteristic-zero blocks at their actual levels; tex 9638–9646 fixes the coefficient ring for the divisions. The existing cuspidal old/new spanning and newform number-field modules give a route for the cuspidal part. The cuspidal local sectors and Eisenstein divisor-sum/local sector proofs take constituent data and columns as inputs and do not construct the holomorphic weight-two Eisenstein space. No analytic weight-two Eisenstein decomposition with these labels was located.
- VERIFIED: On this base the vendored completion extension and norm theorems are available. The norm on the receiving completion restricts to the base norm raised to the local degree, so the fixed valuation comparison needs normalization. The field completion and algebraic complex embedding have different roles.
