**Source inspection on 2026-11-05:** ## A008: semisimplicity and scalar commutant

Consumer: lines 694-710. Sources: Faltings 1983, section 5, Satz 3 and Satz 4, printed p. 360, and the 1984 erratum, printed p. 381. Both page images were checked. Satz 3 asserts semisimplicity of the rational Tate representation over a number field. Satz 4 identifies the integral endomorphism ring tensored with Z_l with Tate-module endomorphisms; its rational consequence is the one used here. The erratum replaces the constant-height assertion in Satz 2 by eventual stationarity and explicitly says that this suffices for the later results. It does not withdraw Satz 3 or Satz 4.

Here A is over the number field L', and every geometric endomorphism is already defined over L, hence over L'. The non-CM branch assumes End^0(A_bar)=B. Thus End_G(V_l(A))=B tensor Q_l. Scalar extension commutes with this commutant: it is the simultaneous kernel of linear commutator maps, and a finite subfamily already cuts out that kernel. The finite-dimensional algebra generated by the Galois image acts semisimply. In characteristic zero its semisimple quotient is separable, so semisimplicity persists under coefficient extension, including an algebraic closure.

After splitting B, write V=r plus r. Its Galois commutant is M_2(End_G(r)), whereas Faltings gives M_2(E). Consequently End_G(r)=E, and after further scalar extension the same argument gives the scalars. A reducible semisimple module would have a nontrivial projection in its commutant. Hence r restricted to G_L' is absolutely irreducible, and so is r on the larger group. This uses semisimplicity as well as the scalar commutant; there is no claim about residual irreducibility.

Accept conditional on A007/A061/A062 and the established non-CM endomorphism algebra. No new bound or field extension is required.
