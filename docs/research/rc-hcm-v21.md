**Verdict.** The direct route fits the four-input limit. This contribution proves the ideal action and several local reduction steps, and gives the oriented correspondence its own statement. The geometric specialization from the actual Heegner point on $X_0(N)$ to the adelic optimal-embedding class remains GLUE. No existing theorem, hypothesis or registry is changed.

## Known issues

No mathematical error in the checked manuscript steps or a printed source was verified. The old Lean multiplicity-one theorem gives an abstract sign-fibre bijection; it does not itself identify the geometric reduction of the actual enhanced curve. That distinction is already visible in the separate `cm_class` field at `ZInQKernel/Contrib/OneProof/RC/PMGeometry/Heegner.lean:37–44`. Equality of $j$ gives a geometric isomorphism; twists preclude asserting descent to an arbitrary original model field. Keep the place fixed when enlarging the field. Arithmetic Artin acts by the inverse ideal on the lattice; inversion has no effect on the final sum with the quadratic character (tex 9292–9295). The two inert residue orientations must not be combined in the reference orbit.
