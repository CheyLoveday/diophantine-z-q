# The rational model of X₀(N)

**Scoped stop.** For every nonzero `N`, this lane constructs and proves smoothness and properness of the normalization of the projective j-line in `ModularCurve.modularFunctionField N`. It identifies the function field with the specified rational Laurent-series field, constructs its formal q-cusp and a complex point for each upper half-plane parameter, and proves that every regular section evaluates on the chart by a single meromorphic continuation. The `X0Model N` constructor remains open: the normalized-branch orbit theorem and convergence of the Laurent expansions of rational expressions are still missing. No new print or assumed Lean result was added.

**Citation discrepancy.** The pinned manuscript does not directly cite Shimura 1971 for this model, either at the three passages below or in its bibliography. Its Shimura citation at 770–771 and 11053–11057 is to the 1973 paper for a different step. The other branch records a secondary reference from that article to Shimura's 1971 book for the standard model (locator and source reading UNVERIFIED). The construction here uses the manuscript's requirements and theorems already in the tree (provenance (c)).

## Exact remaining inputs

1. **Orbit comparison for the normalized branches** (tex 2915–2919, the lattice and level orientation underlying the choice of points; owner: missing theory, substantial). Identify the projection of the valuative lift with the finite first coordinate `j(τ)` and use its analytic quotient description: `j(τ) = j(σ)` iff `σ = γ • τ` for some `γ ∈ SL₂(ℤ)`. For each `τ`, over `j(τ)` compare the branches of the normalization labeled by `Γ₀(N)γ` using the local parametrizations `w ↦ (j(w), j(Nγ • w))`. The specializations of the two labeled germs coincide exactly when their labels are in the same right orbit of `Stab_SL₂(ℤ)(τ)`. This is a concrete statement about branches of the modular equation and the stabilizer, independent of rational sections and q-expansions. Together with the first-coordinate description it gives the required point equivalence. A proof must compare normalized branches with local monodromy around `j(τ)`; using equality of the two plane coordinates instead loses the distinction at collisions. No statement of this comparison was added as a print.
2. **Convergence of the prescribed Laurent series for a rational expression** (tex 2837–2838 and 2915–2919, to compare the algebraic chart with its analytic use; the series compatibility is explicit in `RC/PreparedParam/Prints.lean`, not in those tex lines; owner: missing theory, medium). For every `a : modularFunctionField N`, take the meromorphic function `g` in normal form with punctured germ `fieldGerm N τ a` at every `τ`, as constructed by `exists_meromorphicContinuation`. Prove that there is a `T : ℝ` such that for every `τ : UpperHalfPlane` of height above `T`, the bilateral series with terms `(a.coeff n : ℂ) * exp(2 * π * I * n * τ)` has sum `g(τ)`. This involves no schemes, sections, or orbit assertion. The generator sums are already available; addition and multiplication give the polynomial sums. The missing closure step is inversion of a nonzero denominator: factor off its leading q-power, invert the analytic unit on a small q-disk, and match its Taylor coefficients with the inverse of the formal power series. `sectionMeromorphicContinuation` then supplies every other clause of `sectionExpansion`.

After those inputs the same rational curve and chart give all fields of `X0Model N`. `CMFieldOfDefinition` (the existing print) and `AlgebraicParametrizationAtScale` (the sibling lane) are separate downstream inputs; neither was used here.
