# Modular parametrization coordinates in the rational function field

Work in progress. The target is the bilateral Laurent expansion proposition in the at-scale report.

## Known issues

- In the proposed coset argument, the symmetric coefficients are rational functions of (j) in general. The stronger polynomial claim would require that they have no poles in the upper half-plane. For example, already at level one, (1/(j-a)) for a value (a) attained by (j) has an interior pole. Use the full field (mathbb C(j)) for the degree argument.
- The audit of tex 1781–1788 in the at-scale report records that Milne's cited pair covers the map and quotient, but the normalized newform has a separate locator and the cited pair does not supply the stated Hecke equality.
