## 1. The modular-curve part: `X0RatStructure ⇐ X0ModuliGalois` (no CM)

`X0RatStructure` (tyz-split): for `g` with `IsRatModFn N g` and `τ, τ'`, if the moduli coordinates
`modCoord N τ i` (`i = 0`: `j(τ)`; `1 ≤ i < N`: elementary symmetric functions of the Weber values
`h(k/N)`, `1 ≤ k < N`) are algebraic and `ρ ∈ Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)` maps them to those of `τ'`, then `g(τ), g(τ')` are
algebraic and `ρ`-conjugate.

**Leaf `X0ModuliGalois`.** For `N ≥ 1`, `g` with `IsRatModFn N g`, `σ : ℂ ≃+* ℂ`, `τ, τ'`, `μ`: if the
moduli coordinates of `τ` are algebraic, `σ(g₂(τ)) = μ⁴g₂(τ')`, `σ(g₃(τ)) = μ⁶g₃(τ')` (`IsConjModel`:
`(x, y) ↦ (μ⁻²x, μ⁻³y)` is an isomorphism `E_τ^σ ≅ E_τ'`), and (for `N ≥ 2`) for some `1 ≤ k < N`,
`σ(℘_τ(1/N)) = μ²℘_τ'(k/N)`, `σ(℘'_τ(1/N)) = μ³℘'_τ'(k/N)`, then `σ(g(τ)) = g(τ')`.
Why it is a consequence of the print, never stronger: the hypothesis says that the isomorphism sends `σ(P)`,
`P = ξ_τ(1/N)` the generator of `C_τ = ⟨1/N⟩`, to `ξ_τ'(k/N)`; since `σ(P)` has exact order `N`, so does
`k/N`, hence `(k, N) = 1` and `(E_τ, C_τ)^σ ≅ (E_τ', C_τ')`; the conclusion is the Galois equivariance
`g(x)^σ = g(x^σ)` of the canonical model `X₀(N)_ℚ` (the model whose function field is the field of
functions with rational `q`-expansion at `∞`, the cusp `∞` being the Tate curve with `μ_N`, which matches
`⟨1/N⟩`) at the `ℚ̄`-point `x = (E_τ, C_τ)`. The algebraicity hypothesis was added after review (it makes the
leaf the `ℚ̄`-point form; both uses supply it).

**Proof of the reduction** (`x0RatStructure_of`, `moduli_transport`; VERIFIED):
* `σ ∈ Aut(ℂ)` extending `ρ` (`autExtends`, proved: `exists_ringEquiv_extend`, Steinitz with Mathlib's
  transcendence bases and `IsAlgClosure.equivOfEquiv`);
* from `σ(j(τ)) = j(τ')` and `Δ ≠ 0`, a model `μ` (`exists_conjModel_of_jL`, three cases `j = 0, 1728`,
  generic); `discNeZero` is proved (`Disc.lean`: `G_k(⟨1,τ⟩) = 2ζ(k)E_k(τ)` from Mathlib's
  `tsum_eisSummand_eq_riemannZeta_mul_eisensteinSeries`, `ζ(6) = π⁶/945` via `bernoulli 6 = 1/42`, and
  `g₂³ − 27g₃² = (64π¹²/27)·1728·Δ(τ) ≠ 0` from Mathlib's `discriminant_eq_E₄_cube_sub_E₆_sq`,
  `discriminant_ne_zero`);
* from the elementary symmetric functions, the multiset of Weber values is carried by `σ` onto that of `τ'`
  (`multiset_eq_of_esymm`, Vieta + `roots_multiset_prod_X_sub_C`), so `σ(h_τ(1/N)) = h_τ'(k₀/N)`; the model
  is adjusted by `μ ↦ iμ` (`j = 1728`), `ω μ`, `ω² μ` (`j = 0`) to get `σ(℘_τ(1/N)) = μ²℘_τ'(k₀/N)`
  (`IsConjModel.adjust`), and `℘'` follows up to sign from Mathlib's `derivWeierstrassP_sq`
  (`k = k₀` or `N − k₀`);
* algebraicity of `g(τ)`: the leaf at `τ' = τ` for every `σ` fixing `ιC(ℚ̄)`, and `fixedAlgebraic` (proved:

## 2. The CM part: `CMModuli ⇐ ShimuraMainCM + ArtinLift + IdeleLatticeScale`

**Leaf `ShimuraMainCM` (Shimura 1971, Thm 5.4, printed form).** Let `K = ℚ(√−m) ⊂ ℂ` (through `κ`),
`e : Kˢ → ℚ̄` normalized, `σ ∈ Aut(ℂ)`, `s ∈ 𝔸_K^×` (Mathlib's `IdeleGroup`) with `σ = [s, K]` on `K^ab`
(`ArtinCond`: for every finite abelian `F ⊂ Kˢ`, `σ` acts on `ιC(e(F))` as Tau Ceti's
`ideleClassArtinMap K F [s]`), `𝔞 ⊂ K` with `κ(𝔞) = Λ_P`, `Λ_{P'} = κ(s⁻¹𝔞)` where
`s⁻¹𝔞 = {x ∈ K : s_f x ∈ 𝔞̂}` and `𝔞̂` is the closure of `𝔞` in Mathlib's `FiniteAdeleRing (𝓞 K) K`. Then
`∃ μ`, `IsConjModel σ P P' μ` and for `u ∈ K \ 𝔞` and `v ∈ K` with `s_f v − u ∈ 𝔞̂` (i.e. `v` represents
`s⁻¹u`): `σ(℘_P(u)) = μ²℘_{P'}(v)`, `σ(℘'_P(u)) = μ³℘'_{P'}(v)`.
Faithfulness (review: OK): Tau Ceti's Artin map sends a uniformizer at `𝔭` to the arithmetic Frobenius
(`rayClassQuotient_ofAdicCompletion_of_isUniformizer`, `isArithFrobAt_rayClassArtinMap_idealClass`, read),
which is the normalization under which the print reads `E^σ ≅ ℂ/s⁻¹𝔞` (`E^{Frob_𝔭} ≅ ℂ/𝔭⁻¹𝔞`, Silverman,
*Advanced topics*, II.4.3, UNVERIFIED number; consistent with the kernel's `MainTheoremCMj`); every analytic
isomorphism `ξ' : ℂ/s⁻¹𝔞 → E^σ(ℂ)` with `ξ'(0) = O` is `(μ²℘_{P'}, μ³℘'_{P'})`; restricting to `u ∉ 𝔞` only
weakens the print.

**Glue `ArtinLift`.** Every `σ ∈ Aut(ℂ)` fixing `κ(K)` satisfies `ArtinCond σ s` for some idele `s`. True
(surjectivity of the Artin map `C_K → Gal(K^ab/K)`: dense image plus compactness of `C_K` modulo its
connected component; Neukirch VI §5, UNVERIFIED). Tau Ceti has finite-level surjectivity
(`ideleClassArtinMap_surjective`) and the absolute Artin map with dense image only; the compactness step
(finite class number + compactness of `Ô^×`) is missing.

**Glue `IdeleLatticeScale`.** Under the row hypotheses and `ringClassIdeleQuotient O [s] = picClass J`:
`κ(s⁻¹𝔞) = c'·⟨1, τ_{Q'}⟩` and `κ(s⁻¹𝔞_N) = c'·⟨1/N, τ_{Q'}⟩`, `𝔞 = κ⁻¹⟨1, τ_Q⟩`, `𝔞_N = κ⁻¹⟨1/N, τ_Q⟩`.
True (review: OK): `s = k·s_J·w` with `w ∈ Ô^×ℂˣ`; both lattices are `κ(O)`-stable (kernel
`kappa_mul_mem_lat`, `kappa_mul_mem_latN_gen`), so `s⁻¹(𝔞, 𝔞_N) = k⁻¹(J ∩ O)⁻¹(𝔞, 𝔞_N)`, and localizing the
hypotheses gives `c' = (κ(k)c)⁻¹`. No CM theory. Needs: the kernel of Tau Ceti's ring-class quotient in the
form `Kˣ·Ô^×·ℂˣ` and the local description of `K ∩ x⁻¹𝔞̂` for ideles `x` prime to `f`.

**Proof of the reduction** (`cmModuli_of`, `ringClass_of_artin`, `ideleLatticeHeegner_of`; VERIFIED):
