# Laurent expansions and normalized branches of X₀(N)

**Scoped stop.** For every nonzero level, the prescribed Laurent series of every modular function converges to its meromorphic continuation near the formal q-cusp. This gives the full `sectionExpansion` field. The complex chart is constant on Γ₀(N)-orbits, its projection is the analytic j-value, and the fibers of analytic j are exactly the full modular-group orbits. These facts give the complete `PreparedParam.X0Model 1`. For general level the sole open step is to show that two sheets with the same point on the normalization match on a punctured neighborhood after a stabilizer reparametrization. There are no new prints, axioms, or admitted results.

## Exact remaining input

**Normalized branch comparison** — tex 2915–2919; owner: missing theory, substantial local comparison. For a fixed nonzero N, write `J_N(w) = j(Nw)` and `P_N(w)` for `X0Model.complexPoint N w`. The one remaining implication is:

> For every upper half-plane parameter τ and γ₁, γ₂ ∈ SL₂(ℤ), if `P_N(γ₁ • τ) = P_N(γ₂ • τ)`, there exists s ∈ SL₂(ℤ) with `s • τ = τ` such that `w ↦ J_N(γ₁ • w)` and `w ↦ J_N((γ₂ * s) • w)` agree as punctured germs at τ.

The precise Lean proposition below is a specification of the missing theorem, not an added hypothesis or declaration:

```lean
∀ (N : ℕ) [NeZero N] (τ : UpperHalfPlane) (γ₁ γ₂ : SL(2, ℤ)),
  X0Model.complexPoint N (γ₁ • τ) = X0Model.complexPoint N (γ₂ • τ) →
  ∃ s : SL(2, ℤ), s • τ = τ ∧
    (fun w : ℂ => X0Model.analyticJN N (γ₁ • UpperHalfPlane.ofComplex w))
      =ᶠ[𝓝[≠] (τ : ℂ)]
    (fun w : ℂ => X0Model.analyticJN N ((γ₂ * s) • UpperHalfPlane.ofComplex w))
```

This statement does not assert any level-subgroup orbit. It compares two centered lifts on one j-fiber and supplies the one direction where specializations can lose plane information. The converse direction, the first-coordinate classification, and the conversion of germ matching to the right stabilizer orbit are proved. With the comparison, take γ₁ = 1 and choose γ₂ by `sl_orbit_of_complexPoint_eq`; the right orbit then gives the missing implication in `complexPoint_eq_iff`. The remaining fields are assembled as in `modelOne`.

One route is to prove overlap of the two lifted parametrizations on punctured neighborhoods in a single complex analytic chart at their common normalized point. The algebraic/analytic step is to identify the specific valuative lifts with holomorphic maps into such a chart and prove their local openness. Continuity and equality of the first projection alone do not ensure overlap of the lifts. The tree has affine complex-point topology and smooth complex charts in `CloudB3W17Hodge/Port/Lemmas/AlgebraicGeometry/ComplexPoint/`, and an actual analytification point dictionary in `CloudB3W17bOkaComparison/OriginalPointEquiv.lean`. Neither was connected to the modular valuative lifts here. One can work over the finite affine j-chart, base change to ℂ, and use evaluations of its regular sections and the canonical étale coordinate at the common point. An open image of each nonconstant holomorphic lift provides the requisite overlap in the normalization. Projecting that overlap to the plane and taking the finite local stabilizer gives the germ match. Equivalently one can prove the same comparison by identifying the normalized points with the orbits of local monodromy in the finite cover; this requires identifying that cover with the convergent branches. Mathlib's `ModularGroup.stabilizer_I` and `ModularGroup.stabilizer_ρ` give the four and six matrices at the two elliptic points; the center-fixing action in the proved neighborhood statement includes both. A coincidence of plane values at the center does not implement this route.
