# Conductor-one CM reduction at the detector

**Scoped stop.** The fixed-type source and the specified finite adelic orbit have independently computed ideal-class actions. Lean proves the equivariant bijection between these models from a comparison on prime-to-$qN$ ideal quotients at the reference point. The missing geometric input is the **labelled supersingular curve / quaternion order dictionary at the detector place**. The existing unlabelled class-set equivalence cannot identify the tangent character or read an ideal quotient in those adelic coordinates. Thus the geometric instance of `OrientedCorrespondence` remains open. The new pointwise consumer is in `HCMV21/Consumer.lean`; the old theorem and its consumers are unchanged.

## The missing geometry and its consumer

There are no new abstract mathematical hypotheses registered as leaves. `IdealLatticeClass` is the concrete fractional ideal quotient. The adelic orbit is the range of the finite idele map into a double quotient, and `hunit` concerns only the intersection of local units. The comparison on coprime representatives is an equality at the single reference point; it says nothing about injectivity, surjectivity, or reduction at every point. The proof of the full statement uses both independently computed orbit maps and the ideal CRT.

The smallest quaternion statement still needed is the geometric refinement of the unlabelled `GeometricInput.classes` at tex 9136–9169 and 9265–9279. For a supersingular enhanced pair $(A,C)$ in the selected geometric residue field, the refinement must identify its completed endomorphism order $\operatorname{End}(A,C)_p$ with the left order of the associated locally principal right ideal. It must identify the action on the chosen split-level summand with the respective Eichler projector, and the scalar on $\operatorname{Lie}(A)$ with reduction of the division order at its maximal ideal at $q$. For an embedded maximal quadratic order and an integral $\mathfrak a$ prime to $qN$, the labelled quotient $A\to A/A[\mathfrak a]$ must then have right-ideal class read by multiplication with the corresponding finite idele. This is an assertion about **supersingular curves and ideals alone**. It does not say that any CM curve reduces to a given target or assert that reduction is equivariant.

From that dictionary, the local split-lattice normal forms (including the chosen level projectors) and the division-order uniformizer calculation give the local cosets $F_p^\times z_{0,p}R_p^\times$. One still needs to show that the prescribed tangent character picks the even valuation at $q$, and to assemble these cosets in the restricted product. The order intersection then has unit group $\widehat{\mathcal O}^\times$ by maximality and the integral closure computation. Rational conjugacies of **embeddings** must fix $F$ pointwise. Allowing the normalizer loses the labelling by allowing conjugation of $F$ and can switch the tangent character.

For the CM side there is a separate weaker construction: spread the actual elliptic curves, level subgroups, isogenies and order generators over a common finite field of definition and extend the maps on their good models over the detector place. Show that the special quotient is the quotient by the specialized prime-to-$qN$ kernel, and read its scalar on the tangent line. The prior extension, finite étale, kernel comparison and inert supersingularity lemmas supply parts of this construction. The differential on each generic fibre must be compared to the selected embedding; equality follows after reduction at the same place. These steps do not identify any class in the quaternion class set.

With these inputs, apply `orientedCorrespondence_to_adelicOrbit` with the actual geometric reduction, the chosen reference $x_0$, and the modulus over $qN$. Its bijectivity gives multiplicity one in the selected fibre. For the detector use `point_reduction_of_orientedCorrespondence`: from the reference equality $\mathrm{red}(x_0)=z_0$, compute the supersingular class $[a z_0]$ of its translate, then apply the **separate** parametrization square. The theorem supplies $\pi_{\rm sp}(z_a)=\mathrm{red}(y_a)$ and its `InputsAtDatum` form can be used with the unchanged `lamOf_reduction_at` to obtain the weighted sum at tex 9279–9295. No factor of the class number occurs.

## Known issues

No manuscript or source error was verified. In the pinned formalization, a sign-fibre bijection and the geometric class of the reduced reference point are different claims. Also, a geometric variable change between two equal-$j$ equations supplies no descent to a prescribed local field. The place and complex type must both be fixed; choosing either independently at each class invalidates the residue-orientation argument. The ideal convention used here is $I\mapsto\mathfrak a^{-1}I$ on lattices and left multiplication by the idele of $\mathfrak a$ on the target. Reindexing by inversion in the final quadratic sum is a separate matter.
