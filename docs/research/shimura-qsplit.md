# Shimura Theorem 1: quotient split stopped

**Scoped stop — error in the proposed Lean quotient input** (see the
[committed verdict](../../.fleet/shimura-qsplit-verdict.md)). At
`diophantine_zq.tex` L770–773, the manuscript uses Shimura's quotient
construction. The requested `ShimuraQuotientByC` can reject even a genuine
quotient by Shimura's `C`: an unconstrained dummy index can make
`PrintQuotient` require that its surjective quotient map be zero. No Lean
declarations were added, so there is no new print, leaf, fold, or count change.

## Counterexample to the proposed interface

Given a genuine printed triple `(A, ν, θ)` for `ξ`, keep it and keep all
positive-index operators. Put `ξ' 0 = 1` in `AbVar.End J`. Both
`XiAction ... ξ'` and `PrintedClauses ξ' f ν θ` still hold, since the
only tests on the operators in either predicate use `0 < n`. The
mathematical `C` built from the positive-index Hecke operators is the same.

The definition of `printRing ξ' f` generates a subring using all
`n : ℕ` ([ShimuraSteps.lean](../../ZInQKernel/Contrib/OneProof/ShimuraSteps.lean#L375)).
The cusp form has `ak f 0 = 0`, as used by `xi_mem` at L193–197.
The ring therefore contains `(1, 0)`, so the identity belongs to its
annihilator and also to its saturation (take the multiplier to be `1`).
Applying the killing conjunct of `PrintQuotient` to the identity gives
`ν = 0`. The dimension clause gives `dim A = [K_f : ℚ] > 0`:
the coefficient field contains `ℚ` and is finite dimensional for the
printed triple. The zero homomorphism factors through the identity point of
`A` and cannot be surjective onto such an `A`. The failure is already in
the killing conjunct.

`HeckeRelations.zero` is already used in `xi_mem` to deal with exactly
this index; `XiAction` does not imply it. The fixed
`ShimuraThm1AsPrinted` also quantifies over every `ξ` with `XiAction`
and asks for `PrintQuotient`. Even if the split input supplied the
construction for the normalized operators, the requested reconstruction
would still ask for a witness for `ξ'`, and no triple over `ξ'` can
satisfy both `PrintedClauses` and `PrintQuotient` in this instance.

## Statements and remaining work

- Requested `ShimuraThm1Printed : Prop`: the fixed
  `ShimuraThm1AsPrinted` with only the `PrintQuotient` conjunct deleted.
  This encodes the coefficient field's finiteness, printed clauses (i)–(iii)
  for the weight-two newform on Γ₁(N), and uniqueness of the abelian
  variety up to an isomorphism over ℚ. Index zero is immaterial to this
  proposed statement.
- Requested `ShimuraQuotientByC : Prop`: with the binders through
  `XiAction` unchanged, every triple with `PrintedClauses ξ f ν θ`
  satisfies `PrintQuotient ξ f ν`. This is the statement rejected above.
- The conversions `asPrinted_of_split`, `printed_of_asPrinted` and the
  consumers `shimuraThm1Af_of_split`, `shimuraThm1Af_of_split_closed`
  were left unwritten under the stop rule.

The intended construction is proof content of Shimura 1973, p. 526,
provenance (c), GLUE. The mathematical argument takes the quotient of
`J` by the abelian subvariety `C` generated by the annihilator images.
For a printed triple, (ii) implies `C ⊆ ker ν`. The dimension calculation
in the construction gives `dim(J/C) = [K_f : ℚ] = dim A`. The induced
surjection `J/C → A` is then an isogeny; the connected-kernel condition
in (i) makes it an isomorphism. This gives the universal property for each
genuine printed triple.

**ON THE SPOT (provenance (c)).** The zero-index counterexample and the
interface repair concern Lean's indexing, which neither the manuscript nor
its citations use. The smallest faithful version of the input with the
existing `PrintQuotient` adds `ξ 0 = 0` to the proposed hypotheses. In
that case the zero generator is `(0, 0)`, which is already in every
subring. To use such an input while keeping the old proposition fixed,
the new consumer could bypass `asPrinted_of_split`: obtain the family
from `shimuraHeckeRelations` (via `pointDictionary`), get `ξ 0 = 0`
from its relations, apply the normalized construction, then reuse
`isAfQuotient_of_print`, `clauses_of_printed`, and `compatible_iso`
with `sameDimSurjectiveFinite`. This would yield the exact
`R16Thm1b.ShimuraThm1Af` row. Recovering the combined statement by
reconstruction would also require repairing its zero-index scope.
Neither repair was made. Proving a faithful construction input itself
would require substantial further geometry: a quotient of an abelian
variety by an abelian subvariety with the stated universal property and
the dimension calculation for this `C`. That proof was out of scope.
