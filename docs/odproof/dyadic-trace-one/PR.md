## What Lean proves

At each level, choose the finite Brauer class with normalized invariant 1. If a representative reduces to the chosen one below plus a coboundary, lift the correcting one-cochain and subtract its differential. The compatible representatives assemble into a continuous integral cocycle with trace 1. Transport it through the existing oriented line isomorphism. The existing `orientedLocalTrace_kernel` supplies the kernel argument, and `orientedLocalTrace_boundary` gives the vanishing needed to descend the trace to cohomology. [Compatible cycles and trace-one proof](https://github.com/CheyLoveday/diophantine-z-q/blob/1742527c7d4e0217f8fd278588f334787ff59efc/ZInQKernel/Contrib/RC53Assist/PlaceAboveTwo/TraceOne.lean#L90-L193) · [Existing boundary and kernel lemmas](https://github.com/Deicyde/diophantine-z-q/blob/ddf572d40d932c66b98e296454d6268e9caed383/ZInQKernel/Contrib/RC53Assist/DKLocalAdapter/Adapter.lean#L1048-L1075)
