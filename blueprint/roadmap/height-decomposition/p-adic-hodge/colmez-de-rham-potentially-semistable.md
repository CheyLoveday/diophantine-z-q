# Colmez's theorem: de Rham representations are potentially semistable

The *Colmez statement* is the following assertion. Let $E$ be a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$
inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ and $\rho:G_{\mathbb Q_p}\to\mathrm{GL}_n(E)$ a continuous
representation, and let $V=E^n$, viewed as a representation of $G_{\mathbb Q_p}$ over $\mathbb Q_p$
of dimension $d=\dim_{\mathbb Q_p}V$. If $V$ is de Rham, that is,
$\dim_{\mathbb Q_p}(B_{\rm dR}\otimes_{\mathbb Q_p}V)^{G_{\mathbb Q_p}}=d$, then $V$ is potentially
semistable: there is a finite extension $L$ of $\mathbb Q_p$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ such
that the $B_{\rm st}$-module $B_{\rm st}\otimes_{\mathbb Q_p}V$ has a basis whose elements, viewed
in $B_{\rm dR}\otimes_{\mathbb Q_p}V$, are fixed by $G_L$. Here $B_{\rm st}$ is the
$\mathbb Q_p$-subalgebra of $B_{\rm dR}$ generated by $A_{\rm cris}$, $1/t$ and $\log[\tilde p]$:
$A_{\rm cris}$ is the set of sums $\sum_{m\ge0}a_m\omega^m/m!$ in $B_{\rm dR}^+$ with
$a_m\in A_{\rm inf}=W(\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_p}^\flat)$ tending to $0$ $p$-adically, where $\omega$
generates the kernel of $\theta:A_{\rm inf}\to\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_p}$; $t=\log[\varepsilon]$,
where $\varepsilon=(1,\zeta_p,\zeta_{p^2},\dots)\in\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_p}^\flat$ is a compatible
system of primitive $p$-power roots of unity; and $\log[\tilde p]=-\sum_{m\ge1}(1-[\tilde p]/p)^m/m$
for $\tilde p=(p,p^{1/p},\dots)$, Fontaine's $\log[\tilde p]$ with $\log_p(p)=0$. The assertion is
made for every such $\omega$ and $\varepsilon$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is Colmez's Théorème 0.1 at $K=\mathbb Q_p$, for representations of the form $E^n$, in the
definitions of his §0.2: $V$ is semistable over $L$ when $B_{\rm st}^+[1/t]\otimes_{\mathbb Q_p}V$
is isomorphic to $(B_{\rm st}^+[1/t])^d$ as a module with semilinear action of $G_L$, which is the
basis condition above, and the de Rham condition is the form $\dim_KD_{\rm dR}(V)=d$ that §0.2
states as equivalent to its definition. Colmez uses Fontaine's rings, with
$B_{\rm st}^+=B_{\rm cris}^+[u]$, so $B_{\rm st}^+[1/t]$ is the ring $B_{\rm st}$ above; that this
ring is the image in $B_{\rm dR}$ of Fontaine's $B_{\rm st}$, and that it does not depend on
$\omega$ and $\varepsilon$, are proved here. The proof uses potential semistability in the dimension
form $\dim_{B_{\rm st}^{G_L}}(B_{\rm st}\otimes_{\mathbb Q_p}V)^{G_L}=d$, which is proved here to
follow from the basis form. It replaces a statement of the same theorem from Brinon and Conrad's
unrefereed notes. The manuscript cites Berger's paper, which states the theorem over Berger's own
variant of $B_{\rm st}$. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
