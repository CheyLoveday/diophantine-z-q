# Colmez's p-adic periods of an abelian variety, on a proper model

For every prime $p$, every finite extension $F$ of $\mathbb Q_p$, every abelian variety $A$ over $F$
and every proper model $\mathcal X$ of $A$ over $\mathcal O_F$, there is a family of
$\mathbb Z_p$-linear maps from the Tate module of $\mathcal X(\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_p})$ to
$B_{\mathrm{dR}}$, with values in $B^+_{\mathrm{dR}}$, compatible with the action of
$\mathrm{Gal}(\bar F/F)$, whose extension of scalars to $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$ has no common kernel.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is Colmez's Théorème 5.2 (i)–(ii), in his own setting: a proper model of the abelian variety over
the integers, points with coordinates in $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_p}$, and $B^+_{\mathrm{dR}}$ built
from $W(R)[1/p]$ as on p. 632. The de Rham cohomology is kept only as an index set; linearity in it,
the filtration clause and the Riemann relation (iii) are dropped. The model is not required to be
flat: replacing it by the closure of its generic fibre changes neither its points nor the statement.
Given a proper model for every abelian variety, which Milne's theorem above supplies, it is
equivalent to the row it replaces. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
