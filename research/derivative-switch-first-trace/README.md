# The mod-two local trace for the derivative switch

On our continuous Selmer cochains, the local sign-line trace kills coboundaries and agrees on every cycle with the normalized Brauer invariant. For a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field $F$, including $\mathbf Q_2$, this identifies $H^2(F,\mu_2)$ with $\mathbf F_2$. The class of invariant $1/2$ maps to $1$. The cochain extension can vary off cycles. ## First trace and duality comparison

- [`LocalInvariantTwo.lean`](../../lean/Research/DerivativeSwitch/LocalInvariantTwo.lean) proves that $H^2(F,\mu_2)\simeq\mathbf F_2$ on our continuous cochains for a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field $F$. It specializes to $\mathbf Q_2$. The equivalence sends the class with Brauer invariant $1/2$ in $\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z$ to $1$. At this precision $-1/2=1/2$ in $\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z$, so higher-precision sign and transition compatibility require separate proofs.
- [`LocalTraceTwo.lean`](../../lean/Research/DerivativeSwitch/LocalTraceTwo.lean) applies the invariant to the actual completion terms `localCochains` in `arithmeticCompactSupport`. Its local trace extends the invariant on cycles and vanishes on boundaries. Its values away from cycles depend on the extension. The pairing statement concerns $H^0\times H^2\to\mathbf F_2$ of the sign line and the trace of a cycle scaled by a constant degree-zero class. The $H^1\times H^1$ comparison, elliptic Kummer orthogonality and Poitou–Tate duality are open.
- The resulting compact-support cochain trace is conditional on the sum of the constructed local invariants vanishing on restriction of supported global $H^2$. After the local comparison on Jack’s cochains, the global step requires that vanishing, compatibility of the supported-to-absolute map and all completion maps, and control of contributions outside the chosen set. The global duality consumer also needs exactness on the trace kernel and a trace-one class. The comparison back to $\mathbf Q$ must also account for its real place. The local-duality lemma to pursue alongside it is the comparison of the degree-one cup with the norm-residue pairing, before transferring it to $E[2^n]$ and its local conditions. [Global mod-two reciprocity supplier](https://github.com/CheyLoveday/diophantine-z-q/blob/4606b900ec8e5c26dd989d9ba8e8dd18e73e8773/ZInQKernel/Contrib/BrauerT14T15/GlobalT14.lean#L152-L209) · [Finite local norm-residue map](https://github.com/CheyLoveday/diophantine-z-q/blob/4606b900ec8e5c26dd989d9ba8e8dd18e73e8773/ZInQKernel/Contrib/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.lean#L155-L191)
- A concrete first case of the degree-one comparison uses an unramified quadratic character $x$ and the Kummer character $y$ of $b\in F^\times$. For $\beta^2=b$, the multiplicative coboundary of $g\mapsto\beta^{x(g)}$ is the sign of $y\smile x$ times the carry cocycle of $x$ with coefficient $b$. Here the exponents of $x$ and $y$ use representatives in $\{0,1\}$. Formalizing this identity would connect that cup to the supplier's checked carry invariant. The ramified cases are needed for full dyadic perfectness. [Unramified carry invariant](https://github.com/CheyLoveday/diophantine-z-q/blob/4606b900ec8e5c26dd989d9ba8e8dd18e73e8773/ZInQKernel/Contrib/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/CharacterCarry.lean)
