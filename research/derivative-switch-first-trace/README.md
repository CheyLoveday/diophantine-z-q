# From the derivative switch to `RestrictedConverseGenuine`

This work formalizes one ingredient of the restricted converse, Theorem 5.3 of the 11/5 manuscript. The first trace comparison concerns the sign line at the first positive precision, $A_1=(\mathbf Z/2)[t]/(t)$, in $\mathbf F_2$ coordinates. It reuses the local Brauer invariant and the mod-two Kummer comparison vendored in Jack McCarthy's development tree. Coefficient transport to the retained group ring and to higher precisions remains to be checked.

The cochain comparison, first-precision scalar ring and local $H^2$ invariant compiled in development. The final trace and duality module is a candidate awaiting odProof verification.

## First trace and duality comparison

- [`LocalInvariantTwo.lean`](../../lean/Research/DerivativeSwitch/LocalInvariantTwo.lean) proves that $H^2(F,\mu_2)\simeq\mathbf F_2$ on our continuous cochains for a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field $F$. It specializes to $\mathbf Q_2$. The equivalence sends the class with Brauer invariant $1/2$ in $\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z$ to $1$. At this precision $-1/2=1/2$ in $\mathbf Q/\mathbf Z$, so higher-precision sign and transition compatibility require separate proofs.
- [`LocalTraceTwo.lean`](../../lean/Research/DerivativeSwitch/LocalTraceTwo.lean) is the candidate applying the invariant to the actual completion terms `localCochains` in `arithmeticCompactSupport`. Its local trace extends the invariant on cycles and vanishes on boundaries. Its values away from cycles depend on the extension. The pairing statement concerns $H^0\times H^2\to\mathbf F_2$ of the sign line and the trace of a cycle scaled by a constant degree-zero class. The $H^1\times H^1$ comparison, elliptic Kummer orthogonality and Poitou–Tate duality are open.
- The resulting compact-support cochain trace is conditional on the sum of the constructed local invariants vanishing on restriction of supported global $H^2$. The next trace lemma is that exact vanishing, with compatibility of the supported-to-absolute map, each completion map, and contributions outside the chosen set. The comparison back to $\mathbf Q$ must also account for its real place. The local-duality lemma to pursue alongside it is the comparison of the degree-one cup with the norm-residue pairing, before transferring it to $E[2^n]$ and its local conditions. [Global mod-two reciprocity supplier](https://github.com/CheyLoveday/diophantine-z-q/blob/4606b900ec8e5c26dd989d9ba8e8dd18e73e8773/ZInQKernel/Contrib/BrauerT14T15/GlobalT14.lean#L152-L209) · [Finite local norm-residue map](https://github.com/CheyLoveday/diophantine-z-q/blob/4606b900ec8e5c26dd989d9ba8e8dd18e73e8773/ZInQKernel/Contrib/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.lean#L155-L191)
- A concrete first case of the degree-one comparison uses an unramified quadratic character $x$ and the Kummer character $y$ of $b\in F^\times$. For $\beta^2=b$, the multiplicative coboundary of $g\mapsto\beta^{x(g)}$ is the sign of $y\smile x$ times the carry cocycle of $x$ with coefficient $b$. Here the exponents of $x$ and $y$ use representatives in $\{0,1\}$. Formalizing this identity would connect that cup to the supplier's checked carry invariant. The ramified cases are needed for full dyadic perfectness.
