# Mathematical audit notes

These are review derivations, not claims of novelty. Source locators are in
`sources.json`; conclusions below distinguish algebraic deductions from
unverified source applications.

## 1. Literal curve-model connection

Over K=Q(sqrt(2)), let d=3-sqrt(2), epsilon=3-2sqrt(2), and
D0=75-53sqrt(2). Then epsilon has norm one, D0=epsilon^2*d, and

    (x,y) |-> (epsilon^2*x,epsilon^3*y)

identifies y^2=x^3-d^2*x with y^2=x^3-D0^2*x. The displayed generators in
the two sources agree under this map. This is a literal unit change of model,
not merely an analogy between elliptic curves.

For E364, the parameter D is instead a varying positive squarefree integer.
The equation has the same congruent-curve form, but no common rank-one carrier
or uniform height constants follow from that observation.

## 2. One rational compiler variable

Fix a branch of the finite auxiliary algebra and a specialization of the free
parameters. After reduction it is a finite etale Q-algebra generated by k
coordinate functions, with at most N geometric points. For two distinct tuples
r,s, equality of their values under

    zeta_b = r1 + b*r2 + ... + b^(k-1)*rk

is a nonzero polynomial equation in b of degree at most k-1. A fixed list of
1+(k-1)*binomial(N,2) rational integers therefore contains a separator.
This supplies a uniform finite list; a new rational existential parameter b
is unnecessary.

A rational root of the separating polynomial forces the entire corresponding
tuple to be rational: every Galois automorphism fixes the projected coordinate,
and injectivity forces it to fix every original coordinate. Without separation
this implication fails, as the (plus/minus sqrt(2), plus/minus sqrt(2)) control
shows. Filtering equations by gcd and deleting roots violating nonzero guards
must be done for the WHOLE auxiliary tuple, not separately for each constraint.

For Pi(T)=T^d+sum_j (N_j/D)*T^(d-j), define

    Psi(W)=1+sum_j N_j*D^(2*j-1)*G^j*W^j.

When D*G*W is nonzero this equals

    (D^2*G*W)^d * Pi(1/(D^2*G*W)).

If D*G vanishes it is identically one; W=0 is also impossible. A finite shift
by zero or one handles a zero root before reciprocation. Polynomial conjunctions
use sums of squares over Q, disjunctions use products. Reusing W in different
disjunctive branches is valid; independently eliminating conjunctive witnesses
and then silently identifying them is not.

The remaining application check is the finite chart/degree/pivot atlas and its
exceptional branches. Finite-dimensionality alone does not make a witness set
finite, so this construction cannot automatically compress unrestricted E364
healer choices or all integral points on a positive-rank curve.

## 3. A same-place local certificate

At an unramified odd prime, let alpha=v_p(R) be odd, and put

    W=product_i(1+R*(D_i/R^k_i)^2),
    Theta=Tr(W^-1), B=R/Theta^2.

Assume the required nonzero guards. The valuation of each factor is the minimum
of zero and 2*v(D_i)-(2*k_i-1)*alpha, because the latter is odd and hence cannot
cancel with the constant term. If alpha<0, every W^-1 is integral locally,
so B has negative odd valuation. If alpha>0 and at every place above p some
D_i has valuation <k_i, W has valuation at most -alpha at every such place.
Then Theta has valuation at least alpha and B again has negative odd valuation.
A one-sided pole predicate excluding that outcome consequently forces

    there exists a place above p at which every D_i has its required depth.

This is stronger than allowing a different place for each i. The place may
still depend on p; the original global witness tuple remains fixed.

Exact control: in Q(sqrt(2)), R=17, D1=sqrt(2)-6, D2=sqrt(2)+6 and k1=k2=1.
The good places are opposite. Each individual pole input is 440657/12100,
of 17-adic valuation +1. The joint product is W=161/17, Theta=34/161 and
B=25921/68, of valuation -1. This tests only the local obstruction.

## 4. Actual determinant coordinates

For the same strict matrix D0, finite/singular columns AF,AS, actual cycles
and actual closed rows, choose a local complement x and write

    eta=det[v,x], delta=det J,
    theta=det[D0 | AF*x | AS*J*x],
    qF=lambdaF(AS*J*x), qS=lambdaS(AF*x).

The common-strict calculation gives

    uF=-eta*qF/theta,
    uS=delta*eta*qS/theta,
    qS*(delta*uF)+qF*uS=0.

Thus determinant valuation preservation is equivalent to the relevant integral
unit relation on the ACTUAL cross-evaluations. Equal generic ranks, primitive
cycles/rows and det(J) a unit are insufficient. The retained countercontrol has
DF=[[0,pi],[0,0]], DS=[[0,0],[0,pi^2]], J=diag(1,-1), giving
uF=-1/pi, uS=1/pi^2 and delta*uF/uS=pi.

The LATER fork packets already derive, relative to the retained arithmetic
trace/cup/orthogonal-partner construction,

    cF=-kappa*u, cS=kappa*u,
    qS=-qF, delta*uF=uS

in coherent frames. Their determinant-line bridge retains the same possibly
nonunit strict factor on both sides. It must not be discarded separately.
Arbitrary retained integral bases introduce units, so numerical equality of
coordinates is not the invariant assertion.

This corrects the older 39-target packet's scope: the sign is not simply an
unworked algebraic gap. The remaining source obligations include the actual
retained tuple through inner limits, localized inverse existence, nonzero local
trace for rank inference, and universal group-ring descent. An inner rank-one
lemma does not alone establish every dimension-lowering descent step.

## 5. Scalar units are not universal integral units

Let G=(Z/2)^b and e=|G|^-1*sum_g g in Q_2[G]. Put u_b=1+2e.
Every sign character evaluates u_b to 3 or 1, both 2-adic units. But the
coefficients of u_b in the group basis require denominator 2^(b-1).
For b>=2 the element is not in Z_2[G]; no fixed power of two clears all b.
More strongly, for ANY f in Z_2[G], one has f*e=aug(f)*e. Thus if f*u_b is
integral, then 2*aug(f)*e is integral, forcing v_2(aug(f))>=b-1. Allowing a
non-scalar clearing element therefore does not restore a dimension-independent
augmentation budget. This is precisely the kind of uniformity that requires
an actual universal source construction rather than only scalar testing.

This is a counterexample to an ABSTRACT scalar-to-universal inference, not to
Sun's actual construction. Appendix B explicitly works before character
specialization, with unaveraged cochains and common f,d. Those constructions,
not merely the scalar checks, carry the required content.

A second distinction: for Lambda=Z_2[[t]], t is a unit in the localization at
(2), so 1/t is integral there but has no central value at t=0. Regularity in the
retained Lambda-model and the exceptional height-one primes cannot be replaced
by a valuation check in the completed (2)-local ring.

## 6. Prime-pattern completeness

Once the source's reduction-order promises are granted, CRT compatibility is
explicit: gcd(Lplus,Lminus)=4, their 2-adic valuations are 2 and 6, and the
anchor is divisible by 64. The auxiliary exact-valuation congruences must be
compatible prime by prime with both order systems.

The earlier Sun v3 proof does not merely extrapolate finite avoidance. It
retains ALL good ray-class fibres, gets a finite-cutoff lower mass at least
c*(log L)^(-4k), and subtracts a large-prime tail of order C/(L log L).
For fixed k, the latter is asymptotically smaller. L is fixed first, before
counting size tends to infinity. A slow diagonal handles the short parameter.
This closure is coherent conditional on the two analytic estimates. This
review has not independently established the weighted polynomial counting
input or every step of the large-prime sieve.

This gives an E364 lesson: finite survival/elimination tables need a proved
uniform tail or another complete closure argument. No tail estimate for Sun's
prime population automatically applies to E364 source or healer populations.

## 7. Height-exponent sensitivity

Let a be the height-radical exponent; here a=24. Let B be the maximum root
index and Delta the degree-four integer Lucas defect. The inequalities used
in the paper imply the following strict requirements:

    K_Delta > 2*a = 48,
    K_J > a/2 = 12,
    K_scalar > a = 24.

At the actual precisions 64,16,48, the defect feedback is B^(3/2) versus B^2;
the rational-numerator feedback gives log B <= C+3*h(beta); and the scalar
bound grows as |s-1|^(1/2) versus the Lucas lower bound linear in |s|.
These are strict margins, conditional on the exact local divisibility and
height inputs.

The odd-product height identity has separate arithmetic content: local signs
of the abscissa valuations agree, and both real embeddings have abscissas >1.
The product is in Q(sqrt(2)), not its rational norm. Taking a norm can cancel a
pole at one conjugate place against a zero at the other. In general the loss
in product-height additivity is the weighted sum of min(P_v,N_v), where P_v
and N_v are total positive and negative local log-absolute-value contributions.

### Scalar norm-tree precision

For an odd contact valuation R, each node u^2+r0*v^2 has valuation
min(2*v(u), R+2*v(v)), because the two valuations have opposite parity.
Starting with the strict root bound -R/2, the child-bound coefficient c
becomes c/2 on a left edge and (c+1)/2 on a right edge. The seven leaves
have coefficients 1/16,9/16,5/16,13/16,3/8,7/16,15/16, all strictly below
one. Hence v(G_i/r0^k_i)>-R and integrality forces v(G_i)>=k_i for R>=1.
The formal-group identity [h]R_sc=[s-1]H then gives
v_p(s-1)=v_p(h)+v_p(z(R_sc)/z(H))>=48; nonzero integer h suffices and
need not be a p-adic unit. This checks the logical direction of the
scalar divisibility inference, conditional on the formal-logarithm input.

## 8. E364 marked curve and formal depth

For even N>=2 let N*(N^2-1)=D*s^2 with positive squarefree D and positive s.
On E_D: y^2=x^3-D^2*x the marked point is P_N=(D*N,D^2*s). Exact doubling gives

    x(2P_N)=(N^2+1)^2/(4*s^2),
    y(2P_N)=(N^2+1)*(N^2-2N-1)*(N^2+2N-1)/(8*s^3).

For even N these fractions are reduced. Their square/cube denominator base
is exactly B2=2s. Since the three consecutive terms are pairwise coprime,

    all three powerful <=> D divides s <=> 2D divides B2.

The defect D/gcd(D,s) is the product of primes occurring with exponent exactly
one in N*(N^2-1). At odd p|D, putting k=v_p(N*(N^2-1)) gives

    v_p(-x(2P_N)/y(2P_N))=(k-1)/2.

At two the valuation is 1+v_2(s). This is an exact local formal-depth interface,
not a contradiction by itself.

If N+i=b_i*a_i^2, retain the ordered squarefree parts and the marked Kummer
profile of P_N:

    ([b0*bplus], [bminus*bplus], [bminus*b0]).

Doubling kills the mod-2 Kummer class, so 2P_N alone forgets this marking. The
source diagram must retain P_N and its ordered data as well as its double.
On the square-middle leaf N=X^2, the same map is X^4-1=D*Y^2, s=X*Y; this is
the existing quartic-Pell-to-congruent-point bridge.

To import Sun's contradiction mechanism, E364 still needs a source-qualified
global height/growth comparison with an incompatible bound, or another
complete source obstruction. Varying D, varying ranks, and exponentially
growing Pell coordinates prevent automatic substitution into Sun's fixed
rank-one, polynomial-in-index estimates.
