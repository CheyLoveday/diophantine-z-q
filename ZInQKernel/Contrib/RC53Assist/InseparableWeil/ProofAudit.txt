The proof retains the ramification multiplicities in geometric fibres. Their
sum is the degree, and translation preserves them. The correcting Weil
function is the product of the addition functions raised to those
multiplicities. Thus the divisor proof works for arbitrary isogenies over an
algebraically closed field, without requiring Frobenius factorization.
References: Silverman II.3, III.3.5, III.4.8, III.8.2, III.8.6, V.2.3;
Stichtenoth Definition 3.1.8 and Corollary 3.1.14.
