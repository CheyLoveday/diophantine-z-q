polynomial in 12,504 total variables, including one rational input and 12,503
rational witnesses. Its full expansion has 135,721 nonzero monomials and
maximum absolute coefficient 685783949153203534233601, strictly below 2^80.

- **Full handoff:** the exact public snapshot and selected mathematical notes,
  plus the complete unchanged public November 4 revision-2 archive.
- **Current-only handoff:** the same snapshot and selected notes, with the
  historical archive identified by its public URL and checksum.

audited source, reproducibility scripts, verification receipts, proof

and `COVERAGE.md` after extraction. The low-precision research experiments are
clearly marked unadopted and do not define the released source.
